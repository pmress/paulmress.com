About

The long way, on purpose.

The homepage tells the short version — three chapters. This is the long one: every stop between graduating into a terrible economy and leading Marketing AI Strategy at a Fortune 500 company, in order.

The Path

Chapter by chapter

2005–10
University of Louisville

A degree earned the hard way

Earned his BS in Business Administration, Marketing, while working two part-time jobs and carrying a full course load. Along the way he ran a student-founded 501(c)(3), served as VP of the campus tennis club, and was named a Kentucky Colonel — the Commonwealth’s highest honor — by the governor.

2014–16
The Learning House

First real digital marketing seat

Joined as a PPC Analyst and was promoted to Senior PPC Analyst, running six-figure digital campaigns to acquire students for the agency’s university clients out of Louisville.

2015–16
Concordia University–St. Paul

MBA, 4.0, while working full-time

Earned his MBA in Marketing and Leadership with a 4.0 GPA — started the program on academic probation. Proof to himself that he has what it takes when he puts his mind to doing something hard.

2016–18
J&L Marketing

Digital Strategist to Marketing Director

Promoted from Digital Strategist to Marketing Director, leading six-figure omnichannel campaigns for iconic automotive brands — and presenting at two industry conferences along the way, Digital Dealer in Las Vegas and the Automotive Communications Council in Louisville.

2018–21
Anthem, Inc.

Learned to operate inside a Fortune 25

Started in Member Communications, then moved into Performance Marketing and Channel Activation Strategy — scaling from seven-figure to eight-figure omnichannel campaigns acquiring Medicare members inside a highly matrixed, heavily regulated organization.

2021–23
Allstate · Avail

Built an in-house team from scratch

Joined Avail Car Sharing (owned by Allstate) as Paid Media Lead and was promoted to Head of Performance Marketing, building and leading an in-house team across paid search, paid social, and programmatic, with full accountability for a seven-figure budget.

2023–25
Liberty University

Doctor of Business Administration, 4.0

Earned his DBA in Marketing while working full-time. His applied doctoral research examined how management and employees actually adopt AI chatbots inside a retail marketing organization — the question this whole site is now an answer to.

2023–26
Genworth · CareScout

Director, Digital & Performance Marketing

Took full ownership of paid media, A/SEO, lifecycle marketing, marketing analytics, and marketing technology — first as Director of Digital Marketing, then Director of Performance Marketing — while finishing the doctorate on the side.

2026–
Marketing AI

Director, Marketing AI Strategy, Enablement & Governance

Now leads marketing AI strategy, enablement, and governance inside a highly matrixed, regulated organization — applying that same performance-marketing discipline to how a company adopts AI responsibly.
