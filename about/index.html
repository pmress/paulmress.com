Bio

The short version

Before AI governance, there were fifteen years of performance marketing: eight-figure Medicare acquisition campaigns at Anthem, a Fortune 25 health insurer; an in-house team built from scratch at Allstate’s Avail; three years running digital and performance marketing at Genworth’s CareScout. An MBA and a DBA followed, both finished with a 4.0 while working full-time — the doctoral research asking how management and employees actually adopt AI chatbots inside a retail marketing organization, not how the pitch decks say they will.

That research is the day job now: I lead Marketing AI Strategy, Enablement & Governance inside a highly matrixed, regulated organization, applying the same performance-marketing discipline to how a company adopts AI responsibly. This site runs on the same principle — Claude named as a collaborator, not hidden (more on why in Thinking) — with a provenance mark on every page showing exactly how much is mine.
