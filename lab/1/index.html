Can AI replace a CMS?

Dr. Paul tested what happened when AI took over much of the traditional website production workflow.

Supported Updated Aug. 29, 2026

Hypothesis

AI can eliminate much of the production layer when given the right constraints.

Finding

Execution wasn’t the constraint. Judgment was — this page is itself the evidence.

Single-subject test, concluded — not a controlled study. Read this as one practitioner’s evidence, not a general claim.

The artifact isn’t a writeup — it’s this site, live right now. Every page was produced through the pipeline above.

8 pages · ~1 min deployment · Human review required

Worked: AI handled the implementation surprisingly well.

Constraint: Context mattered — without enough of it, AI drifted.

Finding

Execution wasn’t the constraint.
Judgment was.

AI can quickly build what’s requested. The harder question is whether it’s worth building.

Yes — AI can replace a CMS. What made that possible wasn’t the tooling; it was reimagining the ways of working around AI from the start.

Implication

AI doesn’t replace the marketer. It changes where the marketer adds value.

Don’t just add AI to the workflow. Redesign the workflow around AI.
